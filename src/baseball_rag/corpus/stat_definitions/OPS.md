OPS = on-base percentage (OBP) + slugging percentage (SLG). It combines reaching base and total bases per at-bat.

Higher OPS generally indicates stronger combined on-base ability and power. It does not give a complete ranking of players.

Example: an OBP of .350 plus a SLG of .500 gives an OPS of .850.
