WHIP = (walks + hits) / innings pitched. Home runs count as hits; hit batters, errors, and fielder's choices are excluded.

Lower WHIP means fewer hits and walks allowed per inning. It does not distinguish a single from a home run.

Example: 30 walks and 90 hits over 100 innings gives (30 + 90) / 100 = 1.20 WHIP, or an average of 1.2 hits and walks per inning.
