---
title: Walks Plus Hits Per Innings Pitched (WHIP)
category: stat_definition
tags:

source_url: https://www.mlb.com/glossary/standard-stats/walks-and-hits-per-inning-pitched
---

WHIP = (walks + hits) / innings pitched. Home runs count as hits; hit batters, errors, and fielder's choices are excluded.
